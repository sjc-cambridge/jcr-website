Male and Non-Binary Welfare

Alex Mercer

Hi, I’m Alex and I’m your male welfare officer this year. I’m a first year studying Geography and I’m also part of the college football, lacrosse and volleyball teams. My aim is to ensure that all students are supported by organising welfare events, providing anonymous sexual health supplies, and running weekly drop in sessions. If you have any worries or issues at all I’m always available for a chat and I am able to point you towards someone in the college’s support team if you require further help.
