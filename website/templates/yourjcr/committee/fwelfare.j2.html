Female and Non-Binary Welfare

Amara Mulliner

CRSid: aom32

Hi, I’m Amara, your female welfare officer this year. I’m a second year studying Economics, and play netball, hockey and cox for the college. I’m also part of the University Athletics team. My role is to keep College as happy and healthy as possible, running weekly drop in sessions, organising welfare events and providing anonymous sexual health supplies. If you have any worries or issues at all I’m always available for a chat and I am able to point you towards someone in the college’s support team if you require further help.
