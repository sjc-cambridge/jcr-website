Useful contacts and links

There are plenty of services in Cambridge to support you during your time here. Familiarise yourself with what’s available below, and don’t forget – there are plenty of officers on the JCR committee who are very approachable and can point you in the right direction!

Yourself

During your time at John's your new address will be: 

(Your name)
St John’s College
Cambridge
Cambridgeshire
CB2 1TP

Parcels sent to your address will be delivered to the Great Gate Porter's Lodge and you'll get an email as soon as they're processed. Letters will be put in your pidgeon hole in the mail room next door. (You'll need your CamCard to get in!)
