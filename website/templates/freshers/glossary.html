Glossary of Terms

Every university has its own slang, and Cambridge is no exception: welcome to the world of pidges and plodges!

  • The Backs – The grassy area outside St John’s College New Court.
  • Blues – Blues are students who have represented the University in a Full‐Blue sport in a Varsity match against Oxford.
  • Buttery – The Buttery Dining Room (cafeteria) here at St John’s where breakfast, lunch and dinner are served.
  • DoS – Acronym for Director of Studies.
  • Ents – These are parties thrown for students of the College by the St John’s JCR and happen three times a term.
  • Fellows – Fellows are academics of the College.
  • Hall – Going to Hall means going for a splendid three‐course meal, wearing their gowns, in the College’s Dining Hall. This is available to students every day apart from Saturday, should they wish.
  • JCR – This stands for the Junior Combination Room and can refer to one of two things. It either refers to the Undergraduate Common Room in Second Court where the TV/Xbox/ping pong is. Alternatively, it may refer to the St John’s JCR Committee.
  • May Week – The confusingly named week in June, after exams are finished when all May Balls are held.
  • Pidge – Slang for pigeon‐hole where all students mail goes.
  • Plodge – Slang for the Porters’ Lodge.
  • Progress Tests – These will be subject dependent and are usually held at the beginning of Lent (second term).
  • Scholars – Students who achieve a First Class result their Tripos exams are invited to be admitted as a Scholar of the College.
  • Supos – Slang for Supervisions.
  • Swaps – These are student‐organised events where two groups from different Colleges get together and usually go for a meal/drink together.
