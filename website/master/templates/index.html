A warm welcome from the co-presidents

Welcome to the St John's College JCR website!
We hope that this site will give you all the information you need about our fantastic college. We are incredibly proud to be part of an institution with a rich heritage of academic excellence, sporting flair and a huge variety of other opportunities besides. This is a community where the Junior and Senior Combination Rooms work closely together, steeped in over 500 years of tradition, yet progressing with great excitement to see what the next 500 years will hold.
