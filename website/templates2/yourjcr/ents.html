The JCR hosts several parties each term for your ent ertainment. Ents happen on Friday or Saturday nights in the Fisher Building and are the perfect opportunity to dress up and get down to some dirty beats while enjoying ridiculously cheap drinks deals. Entry is very cheap (sometimes free in freshers week) and anyone from the university is welcome so feel free to bring your mates. We sometimes offer discounted queue jump tickets to Fez after the ent as well so you can join the after party. While most of our ents are themed DJ parties, we also have a range of other ents scheduled from Scottish Ceilidhs to Brazilian Samba Nights. With themed cocktails alongside your standard VKs, beers, soft drinks and shots, you certainly won’t be thirsty either. If you have any ideas for ents, contact Lawrence at ents@sjcjcr.com.
