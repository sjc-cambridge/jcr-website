Access

Imy Arden-Jones

CRSid: iaa32

Hi! I’m Imy, a first year medic and your JCR Access Officer. I am also involved in the Cambridge WAMS (Widening Access to Medical School) society and college rowing. My role is to continue the great work of Leia, the previous access officer, in ensuring John’s is accessible for everyone, regardless of background. This includes working on the CUSU shadowing scheme, the alternative prospectus and getting John’s students involved in access work. Check out the JCR Access webpage for more information. If you have any concerns or ideas, or want to get involved in any access programmes, please get in touch!
